Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about stays, billing, or policies? We have compiled responses to typical questions.

How far is Mahima PG from Manipal University Jaipur?

We are located approximately 600–700 meters from the main gate of Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ). This makes it a quick 8-10 minute walk. Additionally, we provide scheduled shuttle drops for students during college entry/exit hours.

Are meals included in the monthly package?

Yes, our room packages include four meals daily (Breakfast, Lunch, High Tea, and Dinner) cooked in our hygienic in-house kitchen. We offer a varied weekly vegetarian menu focused on nutrition and taste.

Are laundry and cleaning services included?

Yes, daily room housekeeping and twice-weekly laundry and ironing services are fully included in all packages with no additional charges.

What is the process to lock in a booking?

You can initiate a request using our online Contact Form, send us a query on WhatsApp, or call our team directly. To secure your room, a booking token advance along with standard student registration documents is required.
Important Guidelines

House Rules

A few simple guidelines that keep Mahima PG safe, peaceful and productive for every resident.

🚭

No Smoking or Substances

Smoking, alcohol, and any substance use are strictly prohibited inside the PG premises.

🌙

Quiet Hours

Maintain silence between 10:30 PM and 7:00 AM so that fellow residents can rest and study.

👥

Visitor Policy

Day visitors are allowed in common areas till 8:00 PM. Overnight guests are not permitted.

🍳

No Cooking in Rooms

For safety reasons, cooking and electrical heating appliances are not allowed inside rooms.

💳

Rent Payment Deadline

Monthly rent is due by the 5th of every month. A late fee applies after the 7th.

🧹

Room Cleanliness

Keep your room tidy and cooperate with daily housekeeping staff for a hygienic environment.

🔧

Report Maintenance Issues

Report any electrical, plumbing or furniture issues to the warden within 24 hours.

🔑

Do Not Share Access

Never share room keys, access cards or PG gate codes with outsiders for your own safety.

🛡️

Respect & Safety

Treat staff and fellow residents with respect. Any harassment or misconduct will not be tolerated.
